# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Early users are a small internal group of up to five people who want to evaluate A-share and Hong Kong stock ideas with an AI-assisted institutional research workflow. They care about the quality of the process as much as the final report: they want to see how a professional investment team frames facts, challenges assumptions, manages risk, and converges on a written conclusion.

## Product Purpose

君宇·投研智能体 recreates a top fund decision room experience. It guides a user from research task setup through fact-base construction, analyst review, long/short challenge, risk review, investment committee convergence, and final institutional-style research report generation.

The product exists to make the normally hidden decision process of professional financial institutions visible, traceable, and easier to review. It is for research assistance and information organization only. It does not provide financial, investment, or trading advice.

## Brand Personality

Institutional, calm, precise.

The product should feel like observing a serious fund investment committee at work. It should avoid generic chatbot UX, overly dense trading terminals, science-fiction visuals, gamified drama, and casual humor.

## Anti-references

Avoid:

- Generic AI chat pages where the user waits for a long answer.
- Overloaded dashboards that put every metric, message, and report section into one screen.
- Cyberpunk trading terminals, neon palettes, purple gradients, glassmorphism, and decorative effects.
- Meme-like agent personalities or theatrical jokes.
- Report pages that look like raw Markdown dumps.

## Design Principles

1. Show the institutional process before showing the conclusion.
2. Reduce cognitive load by guiding the user step by step.
3. Make disagreement legible: every challenge, concession, and risk correction should have a role and reason.
4. Treat the final report as the product artifact, not an afterthought.
5. Keep compliance visible: every analysis and report must state that it does not constitute financial, investment, or trading advice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for text contrast, focus states, and keyboard navigation. Motion must be restrained and respect reduced-motion preferences. Red and green market colors must always be paired with labels or icons so the interface remains usable for color-blind users.
